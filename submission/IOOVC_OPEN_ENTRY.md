## Rubric mapping

### Idea (40%)

Marketplace listings are untrusted. RewardRadar connects canonical source
status, deadlines, acceptance criteria, payout rails, crowding, and effort into
one auditable decision. The product's edge is refusing to turn advertised
money into an unsupported promise.

### Clarity (30%)

The demo follows one question: “Should I spend my next hour here?” It shows
source evidence, the deterministic score, a bounded `plan_pursuit` response,
and the explicit owner-confirmation gate.

### How it works (20%)

The dashboard and the self-hosted MCP surface run from checked-in fixtures;
the Python suite exercises the adapters, graph, safety gates, and no-match
behavior. The public v0.2 commit includes the video and verification packet.

### Polish (10%)

English responsive UI, static export, Fire TV route, readable evidence cards,
visible limitations, and a 94.89-second 1600×900 walkthrough with male
narration. Local checks:
33 tests pass (one optional SDK test skipped), `pnpm lint`, Next static build,
and Vinext client/RSC/SSR build all pass.

## Eligibility and payment notes

The official IOOVC page says the Open category is worldwide, costs US$60 per
project, closes registration on October 31, 2026, and pays winners by January
25, 2027 at the latest. The prize tier depends on total registrations, so
US$50,000 is an upper-tier maximum, not a guaranteed award. Registration,
official-rules acceptance, Stripe payment, and final submission remain
owner-controlled external actions.
